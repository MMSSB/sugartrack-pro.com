Why we built SugarTrack.

Most health apps trap your data in the cloud, bombard you with ads, or force you into subscriptions. We rejected that model.

SugarTrack is engineered as an offline-first Progressive Web App. That means your data stays on your device, the app loads instantly even without an internet connection, and the premium interface feels exactly like a native iOS application.

Meet the Creator

Your Data. Your Rules.

Leveraging modern IndexedDB and LocalStorage APIs, SugarTrack guarantees that nobody—not even us—has access to your personal health metrics.

The Pro Arsenal

Everything you need to monitor, analyze, and report.

Intelligent Analytics

Beautiful, real-time Chart.js visualizations calculate your averages and time-in-range instantly as you log.

Clinical Exports

Generate high-quality bilingual (English & Arabic) PDF medical reports, or raw JSON and Excel (.xlsx) files.

Fluid Square UI

Experience an ultra-premium interface. Draggable bottom sheets and staggered morph transitions on every page.

Dynamic Theming

True dark mode, light mode, and system sync. Personalize your dashboard with the intelligent accent color engine.

PWA Ready

Install SugarTrack directly to your home screen. It behaves, looks, and feels exactly like a native App Store application.

AI Ready Architecture

The codebase is primed for next-gen features, including AI dietary suggestions and continuous glucose monitor syncing.

Google Drive Sync

Securely backup and restore your clinical logs directly to your personal Google Drive account with zero cloud lock-in.

Offline First

All features are fully functional without an internet connection. Your data is stored locally on your device.
