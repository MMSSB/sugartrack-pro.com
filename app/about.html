Pro Features Included

Fluid Square UI

App-like interface with draggable bottom sheets, smooth staggered transitions, and fully responsive layouts.

Clinical Exports

Generate high-quality bilingual PDF medical reports, plus full Excel (.xlsx) and JSON capabilities natively.

Data Analytics

Real-time visual charting, automated AI insights for time-in-range, and calculated variability metrics.

Custom Theming

Complete Dark, Light, and System modes with a robust custom Accent Color engine for true personalization.

Offline Ready (PWA)

Logs and preferences are securely cached locally, ensuring the app is lightning fast and available without the internet.

Privacy First

Your health data belongs to you. Data is stored entirely locally on your device unless you manually trigger an export.

Google Drive Sync

Securely backup and restore your clinical logs directly to your personal Google Drive account.
