Privacy Policy

Last Updated: October 2026

1. Data Collection and Usage

SugarTrack is a local-first application designed to track clinical glucose readings. All health data you enter is stored locally on your device using your browser's local storage. We do not operate external databases, and we do not collect, transmit, or store your personal health information on our own servers.

2. Google Drive Integration

SugarTrack requests access to your Google Drive (https://www.googleapis.com/auth/drive.file scope) solely for the purpose of allowing you to backup and restore your own data securely.

  • The app will only create, read, and modify a single specific configuration file (sugartrack_data.json) that the app creates in your Drive.
  • The app cannot and will not access, read, or modify any other files, folders, or data in your Google Drive.

3. Data Sharing and Third Parties

Your data belongs to you. SugarTrack does not share, sell, or distribute your glucose readings or Google account information to any third parties, advertisers, or external services.

4. Google API Services Limited Use Disclosure

SugarTrack's use and transfer to any other app of information received from Google APIs will adhere to the Google API Services User Data Policy, including the Limited Use requirements.

5. Contact Us

If you have any questions or concerns regarding this privacy policy or how your data is handled, please contact the developer via the official GitHub repository or support channels.
